For the people dancing.

Ryan Redick plays as Ryan R: an Atlanta-raised, Brooklyn-based DJ rooted in the underground, blending hypnotic techno, hard groove, leftfield house, electro and trance into powerful, percussive sets.

An up-and-coming name in the city’s underground, he waits tables by day and lives for the nights behind the decks. DJing is how he met his wife, Abeille ↗, and the two still play back to back. He plays because he loves it, and you can hear it.
